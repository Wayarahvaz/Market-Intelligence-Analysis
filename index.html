PADRAO_REF = re.compile(r"(?<![\d:])(2\d)\s*[-–]\s*(\d{1,3})(?![\d:])")

def normalizar(texto):
    m = PADRAO_REF.search(str(texto))
    return f"{m.group(1)}-{int(m.group(2)):03d}" if m else None

At what margin do we win?

-- Margin of won vs. lost proposals, by size band (current revision only)
SELECT p.faixa_valor,
       d.resultado,
       COUNT(*)                                   AS propostas,
       SUM(p.margem_valor) / SUM(p.preco_liquido) AS margem_ponderada
FROM vw_historico_vendas p
JOIN dim_referencia r ON r.referencia = p.referencia
JOIN deals d          ON d.referencia = r.referencia
WHERE p.tipo_proposta = 'Com margem'      -- pass-throughs excluded
GROUP BY p.faixa_valor, d.resultado
ORDER BY p.faixa_valor, d.resultado;
The answer — a pattern that didn't survive controls
The first number"We win by cutting margin"Once the sources were linked, the weighted margin of won proposals came out far below that of lost ones.
The controlSplit by size, drop pass-throughsLarge projects carry lower margins and dominate the weighted average. Within the band with the most cases, the gap nearly vanished.
The honest conclusionA hypothesis, not a findingOne band, too few cases — to be tested (Mann-Whitney) as the sample grows, instead of forwarding the raw number.
Beyond the chart: question 3 became an operational output — a prioritized worklist of deals without a pricing sheet (negotiating, closed, in progress, pre-pricing, missing reference, no action). It also showed that each company bidding on the same auction lot receives its own spreadsheet, which avoided building an unnecessary mapping table.
Technical decisions & trade-offs
🔑

Business key over system ID

The internal proposal reference (YY-NNN) is the only thing both sources share — normalized with the same regex on both sides.

🧭

A hub against many-to-many

One reference can have several deals (handover copies, auction lots) and revisions. A reference dimension in the middle lets every fact link 1:N.

%

Percentages don't add up

The base ships numerator and denominator — never a pre-divided margin — so SUM / SUM stays right under any filter.

📋

Exceptions become a list

The pipeline never hides a problem nor stops on an expected case: a six-level worklist separates real gaps from normal states.

🔒

Confidentiality by model

Cost and margin stay in a restricted model; sales gets a catalogue generated without them. Hiding a column doesn't protect data.

🗂️

SQLite + Parquet

No server or IT approval (the ODBC driver was blocked); typed columns reach Power BI as-is and port to BigQuery, Athena or Fabric.

The target is reachable: conversion, not demand, is the gap

The business problem: the company was tracking at under a fifth of its annual revenue target with no clear answer for why. Sales data lived in three disconnected places, so leadership couldn't tell whether the gap was a demand problem, a conversion problem, or something happening after a deal was already quoted.
18.9%of the annual target reached; current pace projects ~27% by year-end
2.3%of inbound emails convert to closed revenue
~80%of closed revenue from a single client
72%of lost revenue from internal, controllable causes
Reading the page
  • Demand is not the constraint. The open, qualified pipeline is larger than the remaining gap to target — the problem is how much of it converts.
  • Concentration, not scarcity. One client and two service lines carry almost all closed revenue, so growth depends on diversifying by account and scope.
  • Losses start inside. Most lost revenue traces to pricing and delivery terms, and internal approval cycles longer than the client's own deadline.
  • The market gives a dated reason to act. The 2026–2027 auction cycle (including the first battery-storage auction and transmission lots with BESS) points to where diversification should go.
Decision: an operating-model fix rather than a lead-generation push — named account ownership, the CRM as the single source of truth, cascading sub-targets, and a standing hand-off from Market Intelligence to Sales on which regulated auctions and sector investments to pursue next.
